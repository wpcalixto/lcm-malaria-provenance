# Acquisition notes

- System: low-cost microscopy system developed by students and researchers at Technische Hochschule Mannheim.
- Samples: malaria laboratory cultures maintained at Heidelberg University (not clinical cases).
- Staining: none (acquired without Giemsa staining).
- Images: 8-bit grayscale BMP, 2048 x 1088 pixels, 442 fields in two lists of 221.
- Acquisition parameters (objective, exposure, illumination) are not recorded in the available artifacts.
